Check before treating lawn insects

Damage is not a diagnosis

Yellow or thin turf can come from insects, drought, irrigation gaps, soil conditions, or mowing stress. Start with the full pattern.

Learn more

Inspect at turf level

Look closely at blades, thatch, crowns, and the soil surface. Note where damage starts and whether live insects are actually present.

Treat the confirmed cause

Match the next step to what the inspection finds. Unnecessary pest treatment wastes money and will not fix watering, soil, or mowing problems.

Lawn care basics

Cut consistently, not too short

Mow often enough that each visit removes only a modest amount of growth. Cutting too much at once can leave the lawn stressed and uneven.

Learn more

Read the lawn before treating it

Colour and density can vary because of water, shade, soil, mowing, or nutrition. Check the full pattern before choosing fertilizer or another treatment.

Learn more

Finish carefully around borders

Curved beds, trees, fences, and hard surfaces need controlled trimming and cleanup so the lawn looks finished without damaging nearby plants or edging.

Learn more
