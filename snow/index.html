Know what your quote covers

Confirm the winter service details before you book

Every property and route is different. Your written quote should clearly confirm these details so there are no surprises after the first storm.

  • Clearing areas: driveway, city sidewalk, private walks, steps, and entries
  • Service trigger: the snowfall amount that starts a scheduled visit
  • Timing: the expected clearing window after snowfall ends
  • Plan dates: when monthly or full-season coverage begins and ends
  • Ice management: whether de-icing is included, optional, or quoted separately

Straight answers before you request a quote

How Green Wolf prices snow service

A low advertised price is not useful if it leaves out part of your property. Green Wolf reviews the actual clearing area and puts the agreed scope in writing.

Why is there no single snow price?

Driveway size, sidewalk length, steps, back entries, corner-lot frontage, the service plan, and requested add-ons can all change the work. Your quote is based on the property you want cleared.

What should I send for an accurate quote?

Send the Saskatoon address, the areas you want cleared, the closest property layout, and any access or snow-storage concerns. Clear photos help Green Wolf review the scope before snowfall hides curbs and edges.

Can I request service outside the priority routes?

Yes. Hampton Village, Rosewood, and Brighton receive priority route review. Other Saskatoon neighbourhoods can still request a quote and will be considered based on route availability.
