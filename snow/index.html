Know what your quote covers

Confirm the winter service details before you book

Every property and route is different. Your written quote should clearly confirm these details so there are no surprises after the first storm.

  • Clearing areas: driveway, city sidewalk, private walks, steps, and entries
  • Service trigger: the snowfall amount that starts a scheduled visit
  • Timing: the expected clearing window after snowfall ends
  • Plan dates: when monthly or full-season coverage begins and ends
  • Ice management: whether de-icing is included, optional, or quoted separately
