Straight answers before you request a quote

How much does snow removal cost in Saskatoon?

Green Wolf starts at $159 per month, or $715 for the full season (November 1 to March 31, 10% less than paying monthly). That covers a standard single driveway, the city sidewalk, the front walk and the front steps. Your written quote confirms the exact price for your property.

What changes the price?

A double or extended driveway, a corner lot with extra city sidewalk, a back or side entrance, a rear parking pad, or ice melt. Each is priced in your quote, not added later.

What should I send for an accurate quote?

Just your address and which plan you want. Clear photos of the driveway and steps help us price it before snowfall hides curbs and edges.

Is full season or monthly cheaper?

Full season. Five months at $159 would be $795; the full-season plan is from $715 for November 1 to March 31, a 10% saving. Monthly is for people who want to start later or keep the option to stop.

Do you serve my part of Saskatoon?

Yes. We take snow contracts across Saskatoon. Send your address and we'll confirm your spot on the route with your quote.
