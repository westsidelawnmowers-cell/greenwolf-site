Good to know

The 48-hour sidewalk rule in Saskatoon

The City of Saskatoon's sidewalk clearing bylaw makes the homeowner responsible for clearing snow and ice from the public sidewalk in front of the property within 48 hours of a snowfall. Miss it and the City can issue a warning, then a fine, and can clear it and add the cost to your property taxes.

Both Green Wolf plans include the city sidewalk, so Hampton Village route customers are covered well inside that window without thinking about it.
